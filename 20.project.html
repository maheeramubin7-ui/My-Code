Pasta

1.Boil Pasta: Bring a pot of salted water to a boil. Add pasta and cook according to package instructions. Drain and set aside (save a little pasta water if needed). 2.Prepare the sauce: Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Add garlic and sauté for about 1 minute (don't let it burn). Add tomato sauce, salt, pepper, and chili flakes. Let it simmer for 5-7 minutes. 3.Combine: Add cooked pasta into the sauce. Toss well to coat evenly. Add a splash of pasta water if the sauce is too thick. 4.Serve: Garnish with fresh herbs and grated cheese. Serve hot.

Momo

1. Mix flour, salt, and water to make a soft dough. Cover and rest for 20 minutes. 2. In a bowl, combine minced chicken, onion, garlic, ginger, coriander, soy sauce, oil, salt, and pepper. Mix well. 3. Roll the dough into small thin circles. Place 1 spoon of filling in the center of each circle. 4. Fold and seal the edges to shape the momos. 5. Grease a steamer lightly and place the momos inside without touching each other. 6. Steam for 10–12 minutes until fully cooked. 7. Serve hot with spicy momo sauce or chili sauce.

Cake

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F) and grease a cake tin. 2. In a bowl, mix flour, cocoa powder, baking powder, baking soda, and salt. 3. In another bowl, beat eggs, sugar, oil, milk, and vanilla essence together. 4. Gradually add the dry ingredients into the wet mixture and stir until smooth. 5. Pour the batter into the greased cake tin. 6. Bake for 30–35 minutes or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean. 7. Let the cake cool, then serve or decorate with chocolate frosting.
